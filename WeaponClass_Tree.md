# 🗡️ Weapon Class Tree

## Weapon Types

| Weapon Type | Description              | Advantage               | Weakness                        |
| ----------- | ------------------------ | ----------------------- | ------------------------------- |
| Sword       | Balanced melee weapon    | Beats Axe               | Weak to Lance                   |
| Axe         | High power, low accuracy | Beats Lance             | Weak to Sword                   |
| Lance       | Reach and defense bonus  | Beats Sword             | Weak to Axe                     |
| Bow         | Long-range physical      | Strong vs. flying units | Vulnerable in melee             |
| Dagger      | Fast, high crit          | Great vs. low DEF       | Weak to heavy armor             |
| Tome        | Magic offense            | Ignores DEF             | Weak to RES, anti-magic         |
| Staff       | Healing & support        | —                       | Can't attack                    |
| Spear       | Advanced lance-type      | Strong vs. cavaliers    | Heavy & costly                  |
| Arcane      | Legendary spell type     | High power              | Rare, requires high proficiency |
