## 🗺️ Chapter 1: Forest Ambush

- Size: 12x12
- Terrain: Forests, Plains
- Enemy: 5 Bandits, 1 Boss
- Objectives: Rout all enemies

### Layout (ASCII)

```
P P P P F F F F F F F P
P P P P F . . . . F B P
P H . . . . . . . . . P
. . . . . . . . . . . .
```

Legend:
- P = Plain
- F = Forest
- H = House
- B = Boss Start
